|New industry research by the Alternative Credit Council (ACC): Investor appetite for co-investment has surged: 92% of managers report an increase in investor demand for co-investment (up from ~70% in 2023).
Investor demand for liquidity is rising: 64% of respondents report rising investor demand for liquidity (up from 49% in 2023), with two-thirds of managers surveyed now having at least one vehicle offering investors some form of periodic redemption|
