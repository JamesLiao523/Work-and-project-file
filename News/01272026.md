#### States Lead Private Equity Backlash (Healthcare Focus)

- State legislatures are stepping in to regulate private equity’s role in health care after federal action stalled. Lawmakers in multiple states are advancing bills targeting PE ownership and influence in hospitals and essential providers.

- The push follows high-profile bankruptcies and closures of PE-backed health systems, which raised concerns about quality of care, financial stability, and local health access.

- Proposed laws generally focus on greater transparency, transaction oversight, and limits on non-clinical investor control, including giving state agencies or attorneys general power to review or block deals.

- Some states are also considering restrictions on hospital sale-leasebacks, ownership reporting, and enforcement of corporate practice doctrines to curb influence of private equity management companies.

- Despite this backlash, private equity deal activity remains sizeable, with ~ $100 billion in North American healthcare deals in 2025 — but political and regulatory risk is now part of the sector’s investment calculus.

#### Hedge Funds: Flows & Positioning
- Hedge funds are seeing their strongest inflows in years, with industry AUM reaching record highs, as investors rotate away from traditional long-only equity and bond allocations.

- Macro, equity long/short, and multi-strategy funds are the primary beneficiaries, reflecting demand for downside protection, tactical flexibility, and volatility-driven alpha rather than beta exposure.

- The renewed “Sell America” trade has resurfaced, with hedge funds selectively reducing U.S. exposure and increasing positioning in non-U.S. equities, FX, and rates amid policy, trade, and geopolitical uncertainty.

- Importantly, this is not a wholesale exit from U.S. assets, but a relative positioning shift — hedge funds are hedging U.S. concentration risk rather than calling for a U.S. recession or collapse.

- Overall, hedge funds are increasingly viewed as portfolio shock absorbers, offering diversification at a time when equity-bond correlations remain unstable and macro risk is harder to hedge with traditional assets.
