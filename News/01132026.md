US CLO (Collateralized Loan Obligation) issuance remained robust in December 2025, with $30bn in gross supply. 
For the full year 2025, 1164 US CLOs priced totaling $527bn, up from $495bn in 2024. Private Credit/MM US CLOs accounted for $76.3bn, representing 19% of year-to-date new issuance.

JPMorgan Chase Positioning and Strategic Views
Greater investment allocations to alternative assets continue, yet investors should temper expectations for consistent outperformance. Private assets have underperformed their publicly traded counterparts for three consecutive years and fundraising has been disappointing. Private credit appears to have lost some of its appeal with fundraising slowing to ~$25bn per quarter from ~$50bn peaks, reflecting lower rate expectations; it remains well-capitalized and still has $465bn in dry powder.

Alternatives Strategy Summary

Global markets strategists expect divergent performance and fundraising trends across alternative assets. Overall, they are underweight alternatives relative to publicly traded equities and bonds, reflecting stronger expected returns and liquidity in public markets.
Within alternatives:

* Overweight: Hedge funds and digital assets, supported by stronger demand and consistent alpha generation.
* Underweight: Private equity and private credit, which are expected to lag public market counterparts.
* Neutral / Modest Outlook: Private real estate and private infrastructure, with only modest gains anticipated.

Key Drivers by Asset Class
Private Equity (Headwinds)
* Persistent valuation gap versus public markets
* Slow recovery in distributions, limiting LP liquidity
* Re-acceleration of capital calls, increasing funding pressure on investors

Private Credit (Headwinds)
* Eroding economics in the broadly syndicated loan (BSL) market
* Compressed margins and lower return potential
* Slowing growth in capital allocations

Hedge Funds (Positive View)
* Delivered positive alpha for five consecutive years
* Multi-strategy hedge funds achieved six consecutive years of positive alpha
* Benefit from volatility, dispersion, and active risk management

Real Assets & Digital Assets
* Private real estate & infrastructure: Demand remains, but return expectations are muted
* Digital assets: Stronger fundraising and risk appetite relative to PE and private credit
