#### A leaked draft of the European Commission’s SFDR 2.0 proposal (ahead of its expected November 19 2025 release) outlines a complete overhaul of the existing EU sustainable finance framework.
Key Structural Changes
- Abolition of Article 8 and 9 categories

Replaced by three new product categories:
i) Transition investments
ii) Integration of sustainability factors
iii) Sustainability-related objectives

Each product must meet ≥ 70% alignment, mandatory exclusions, and a defined list of permitted investment types.

- Removal of Principal Adverse Impact (PAI) reporting

Scrapped at both entity and product levels.

Reflects industry complaints about excessive reporting burden and unclear metrics.

- Revised naming and marketing restrictions

New rules on sustainability-related terminology to prevent greenwashing.

Aligns more closely with UK’s SDR labels, but questions remain on potential hierarchies between categories.

- Risk disclosures

Largely unchanged, maintaining focus on transparency.

- Scope reduction

Portfolio managers and financial advisers appear to be excluded from mandatory product-level reporting.
